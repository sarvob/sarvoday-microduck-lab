Real goalkeepers make this look unfairly easy. One step, one dive, save.

Microduck has none of that. No arms. No sidestep. Tiny feet. And once it turns to run across the goal, its own camera can lose the ball.

So I gave myself a small product challenge. Could this duck become a goalkeeper with one compact control idea and a search that fits inside a couple of hours?

First shot. Watch the far post.
