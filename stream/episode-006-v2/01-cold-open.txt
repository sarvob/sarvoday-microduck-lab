Real goalkeepers make this look almost unfair. One step. One dive. Done.

Microduck? Well... it has no arms, no sidestep, and feet roughly the size of two ambitious crackers. Worse, the moment it turns to run, its own camera can lose the ball.

So we made a bet: give this tiny duck a couple of hours and one compact control idea. Could it become a goalkeeper?

Okay. First shot. Watch the far post.
