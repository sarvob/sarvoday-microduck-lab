# Title

Can Microduck Predict the Save? Building a Vision-Guided Robot Goalkeeper

# Description

Can a small robot use its own head camera to predict where a moving ball will
cross the goal line—and get there before it is too late?

In this episode, we turn the authentic Pollen Robotics Microduck into a
vision-guided goalkeeper in MuJoCo. The official walking policy stays frozen.
We add a compact product layer that detects the orange ball from rendered
head-camera pixels, estimates its trajectory, and chooses an interception
target.

The honest result on 15 unseen shots:

- Position-chasing baseline: 14/15 blocks
- Crossing predictor: 15/15 blocks
- Falls: 0
- Goal-zone exits: 0

Repository and reproducible results:
https://github.com/sarvob/sarvoday-microduck-lab

Visual disclosure: the Microduck model, head-camera images, ball motion,
contacts, and evaluation are generated by MuJoCo. Blender supplies an
environment-only opening shot; the arena styling and overlays are presentation
layers and do not affect physics or scoring. The stock camera mount is moved 12
cm forward along its optical axis so it clears the face geometry, and the image
is rolled upright.
