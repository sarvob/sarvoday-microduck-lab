One robot duck learned to swing. We asked what happens when three ducks work together.

Duck one rides. Duck two learns when to push, using the swing angle and velocity. Duck three claps at every peak.

Cross-entropy search tested one thousand and eighty candidate controllers across three seeds. The winning five-parameter policy reached a peak angle of forty-four point eight degrees and sustained eight strong peaks in sixteen seconds.

The swing timing is learned. The applause is synchronized choreography. Every claim has a measurable gate, and the code and results are public.

This is Sarvoday Robotics: open simulations, honest results, and one new robot challenge at a time.
