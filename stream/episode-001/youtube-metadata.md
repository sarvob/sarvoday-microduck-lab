## Title

AI Trains a Robot Duck: 3 MuJoCo Challenges with Public Code

## Description

An AI coding agent trains Microduck to complete three measurable MuJoCo robot simulation challenges: spin in place, sprint through two markers, and push a free ball.

Every result has an explicit success gate. The final controllers completed 1.2 turns with 0.04 m drift, reached both markers in 5.86 seconds, and pushed the ball 1.00 m while remaining upright.

Goal: build robotics skills through reproducible simulation rather than polished demonstrations alone.

Lesson: keep the proven joint-level locomotion policy frozen, then optimize a compact supervisory controller that chooses forward speed, sideways speed, and turn rate.

Code and reproducible results:
https://github.com/sarvob/sarvoday-microduck-lab

Built from the Hugging Face Microduck School workflow and the Pollen Robotics Microduck simulator. Upstream provenance is retained in the repository.

Sarvoday Robotics exists to make modern robotics knowledge more accessible. Subscribe to follow the next open robot challenge.
