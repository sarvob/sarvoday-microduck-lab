# Human narration recording

- Read naturally and calmly; do not sound like an advertisement.
- Leave about half a second between paragraphs.
- Record in a quiet room, 15–20 cm from the microphone.
- Do not say a personal name, email address, employer, or location.
- Target length: 45–55 seconds.
- Preferred source format: WAV, AIFF, M4A, or high-quality MP3.

The final edit will apply noise reduction, gentle compression, loudness normalization,
and timing adjustments without generating or cloning a voice.
