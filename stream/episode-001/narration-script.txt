Can an AI coding agent teach a robot duck new skills—and prove that they work?

We gave Microduck three measurable challenges.

First: spin through one full turn without falling or drifting away. The controller completed one point two turns, with only four centimeters of drift.

Next: reach two offset markers in sequence, forcing a steering reversal. It reached both markers in five point eight six seconds and stayed upright.

Finally: approach a free ball and push it at least thirty centimeters. The learned controller moved it one full meter.

The key lesson is simple: keep the proven locomotion policy frozen, then optimize a small supervisory controller above it. Every result has a clear pass-or-fail gate, and the code and measurements are public on GitHub.
